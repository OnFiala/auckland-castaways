# Offline release check

The **Game release QA** workflow builds the distribution with `python adventure/tools/build_web.py <empty-absolute-directory>`, packages it with `adventure/tools/package_download.py`, verifies the unpacked hashes with `adventure/tools/verify_web.py`, and opens the unpacked root `index.html` in clean offline Chromium profiles. With Python 3.12, Pillow 11.3.0, Playwright 1.56.0 and its Chromium installation, run `python adventure/tests/browser/release_qa.py <unpacked-directory>/index.html <evidence-directory>`. Two ordinary-motion shore visits measure Alick’s leftward gait to the rope and pull the painted wreck block by mouse and touch. A separate reduced-motion campaign plays all seven chapters, including closing and reopening Epigwaitt’s survey and filing the epilogue’s witness accounts and dates, and a disposable copy exercises staying in the current room and retrying a missing painting. The workflow retains the tested ZIP, videos, screenshots, hit coordinates, movement samples and the release number. No server, account, source-game save or credentials are needed.

If the campaign fails, a separate fresh game records overlapping room and workbench controls in `diagnostic/blocked-centres.json`. It uses Tab and Enter on those controls so later chapters can still be tested. The original failure keeps the release check red.

Run `python adventure/tests/browser/animated_route.py <unpacked-directory>/index.html <animated-evidence-directory>` for the complete campaign at ordinary walking speed. A read-only observer records the mounted atlas, visible gait phases and foot positions for Alick, Musgrave and any moving companions in `animated-route.json`. This run also saves screenshots during walking in chapters four and seven, a spoken exchange with a decoded portrait, and the completed archive.

The `keyboard` scenario uses Tab and Enter from a fresh launch to take the rope and pole, combine them, and find the finished tool after reloading. Its JSON records where focus landed after each action and the next Tab. The animated voyage takes a different route on the return: Musgrave marks the landing and speaks to both men before collecting the provisions. It checks his reply at the cutter before sharing supplies and George’s exchange at the landing afterward. Both campaigns play through boarding and the archive.

## Film picture and sound

The release job plays the extracted film in Chromium and exercises interrupted media, bookmarks and reduced motion in Chromium and WebKit. In reduced motion, the first change of camera in the opening must replace one held illustration with the next while the same passage is being narrated. [Hosted playback checks](hosted_film_pass.py)

After assembling the standalone film, run `python adventure/tests/browser/media_cut_parity.py <unpacked-directory> <standalone.mp4> --report cut-parity.json`. FFmpeg decodes eight samples per second from every reel. The check compares the MP4 pictures with the WebM and every frame in the image player. The standalone film must reproduce the sampled MP4 pixels exactly. The sounding lead, hammer, mallet and lantern each have a measurement on the moving object and a second measurement on a fixed feature in the same view.
