UNTIL WE RETURN
A painted point-and-click adventure in seven chapters

Extract the entire ZIP into a folder. Open index.html in a current desktop
browser. Keep the editions folder beside index.html. The game works offline.
For the best chance of keeping your save, use the same browser, the same
folder and a normal browsing window each time. The dot beside the action
line reports whether the browser could save. The hosted game keeps its own
progress in the browser you use to play it.

Click the ground to walk and a person to talk. Pick up an object, then select
it in the satchel and click another object to combine or use it. The magnifier
examines a painting. The eye briefly reveals interactive areas. The question
mark gives a hint in stages. In close-ups you can use the painted parts or
open Working controls. Tab, Enter and Escape work throughout the game.
Three small paintings above the scene show the work ahead. A check marks
each finished objective. Settings shows the release number. Under Scene lighting, Automatic adapts to
your display, Cinematic gives the full moving light, Balanced reduces the
work, and Original painting restores the earlier look.
The opening film follows the Grafton from Sydney to the wreck. At daylight
you take over on the shore. Six short films connect the seven chapters.
The final film follows the rescue. All eight play as part of the story.
Move the pointer, tap the picture or use Tab for pause, sound, captions
and skip. If the browser needs a gesture for sound, English captions appear
and you can choose Enable sound. With reduced motion, the picture stays still
until the next camera cut. The narration, captions and music continue.
If native video stalls, the game can continue with images and audio from
the same edit. Closing the tab saves your place in the current chapter
film. The compass opens Our journey to revisit a film you have reached.
After the ending you can watch the whole film in sequence.

Motion follows your system preference and can be reduced in Settings.
The eight films include their own English voice, music and sound effects.
While you play, a quiet instrumental score changes with each chapter.
A few discoveries and repairs bring a short musical response. Music volume
in Settings adjusts the score and these phrases. Move it to 0 for silence.
Spoken dialogue during play is a separate setting. It starts off and uses
your browser's voice. An installed English voice works offline.

The story follows the Grafton from its wreck in 1864 to the rescue in 1865.
You play Alick McLaren in the first five chapters and Captain Thomas Musgrave
in the last two. The historical notes and the Invercauld papers are available
after the return. The dialogue, puzzles and imagined portraits were made
for this adaptation.
