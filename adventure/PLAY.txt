UNTIL WE RETURN
A painted point-and-click adventure in seven chapters

Extract the entire ZIP into a folder. Open index.html in a current desktop
browser. Keep the editions folder beside index.html. The game works offline.
For the best chance of keeping your save, use the same browser, the same
folder and a normal browsing window each time. The dot beside the action
line reports whether the browser could save. The hosted game keeps its own
progress in the browser you use to play it.

Click the ground to walk and a person to talk. Pick up an object, then select
it in the satchel and click another object to combine or use it. The magnifier
examines a painting. The eye briefly reveals interactive areas. The question
mark gives a hint in stages. In close-ups you can use the painted parts or
open Working controls. Tab, Enter and Escape work throughout the game.
Settings shows the release number. Motion follows your system preference,
and you can reduce it in the game. Brief reaction captions and English
narration start off. Narration uses a voice supplied by your browser.

The story follows the Grafton from its wreck in 1864 to the rescue in 1865.
You play Alick McLaren in the first five chapters and Captain Thomas Musgrave
in the last two. The historical notes and the Invercauld papers are available
after the return. The dialogue, puzzles and imagined portraits were made
for this adaptation.
