# Painted work surfaces

These seven close-ups let the player read a material before acting on it. Each 1536 × 1024 PNG contains the quiet starting state of its puzzle. The paintings are original illustrations for the game. Details such as the notch in the chest and the shape of the mooring post are inventions for play.

| Plate | Description for integration and access |
| --- | --- |
| `chest.png` | A salt-grey chest beneath folded sailcloth. The central iron strap holds the lid tightly. A small splinter has broken out of the joint farther to the right. |
| `roof.png` | Lamplight catches a bead beneath the high edge of an overlapping canvas sheet. A dark trail runs down and right across the fabric below it. |
| `drill.png` | A flattened, worn iron bit rests against a split block. Its deep round trial hole has a loose chip beside it. A brighter, shallow crescent lies farther along the grain. |
| `hull.png` | Looking along the clinker-built side reveals three timber laps. A slim opening interrupts the middle lap between two fastenings. |
| `rig.png` | A sheet hangs slack from the reinforced corner of a small sail. Its frayed end reaches below two wooden fairleads fitted along the rail. |
| `chart.png` | A chart sheet lies between a bound journal and a pair of brass dividers. The worktable looks onto the sea. The bare centre is reserved for the route drawing. |
| `landing.png` | A coil of hemp rests on the thwart, its tail trailing to the right. Beyond the gunwale, small waves lap a weathered post in the shallows. |

The [manifest](manifest.json) records the original prompts, file sizes, SHA-256 digests and measured landmarks. Anchor values are percentages of the original image measured from its top-left corner. Both axes share the same image rectangle, including when the interface letterboxes it. The chart entry also gives the bounds of the clear area for the route drawing.
