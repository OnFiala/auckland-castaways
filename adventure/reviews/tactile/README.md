# Tackle, bellows and rainwater

[Compare six scene-scale compositions](at-work.webp). The left panels show the wreck tackle resting, the cold fitted bellows and the leak inside Epigwaitt. On the right, Alick has pulled the fall, the bellows has driven air into the hot charcoal, and the repaired roof carries water outside.

The comparison uses the running renderer’s stage boxes, reduced-motion contact frames, production paint, character work poses and the actual pan markup. It is an offline composition of the material and player layers. The survey is shown without its controls to keep the rim, water surface and repaired lap visible.

The newly commissioned game illustrations are interpretive reconstructions. The [native PNGs and generation receipts](../../artwork/tactile/manifest.json) and [delivery checksums and registrations](../../dist/assets/tactile/manifest.json) identify the artwork. The two contacts use a finite gesture and return to rest. The roof leak uses a single timed drop and a ring confined to the pan. Repair removes both indoor water marks. Reduced motion holds the material in a single touched position and keeps the roof evidence readable without travel.

Run `node --test tests/stage-life.test.cjs tests/fieldwork-ui.test.cjs` in the adventure directory to check registration, the interaction lifecycle, the roof survey and the still poses. The test fixture drives the room and survey handlers.
